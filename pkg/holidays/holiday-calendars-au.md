# Australia (AU) holiday calendars

Snapshot gathered **2026-11-20**. See the [overall holiday calendar guide](README.md) for date formats, coverage limits, source-confidence grades and Geneos scheduling examples.

Gateway include: `holidays.layer0.au.xml`. Under **Layer 0 - Holidays**, the **Layer 0 - AU - Country** groups hold the named calendars.

Australia has separate state and territory public calendars, including the named city-specific observances. These are not interchangeable with trading calendars: ASX cash, ASX 24 and TMX Australia have distinct schedules. Partial-day holidays, bank-only days and regional exceptions are included or excluded as explained with each calendar, rather than inferred from a national list.
