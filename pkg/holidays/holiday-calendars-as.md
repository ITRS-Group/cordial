# Asia excluding India (AS) holiday calendars

Snapshot gathered **2026-11-20**. See the [overall holiday calendar guide](README.md) for date formats, coverage limits, source-confidence grades and Geneos scheduling examples.

Gateway include: `holidays.layer0.as.xml`. Under **Layer 0 - Holidays**, the **Layer 0 - AS - Country** groups hold the named calendars.

India is documented separately under IN. China currently has only an SSE exchange calendar, not a public-holiday calendar. Hong Kong and Macao retain their own published calendars. Indonesia's collective leave is separate from national public holidays and does not establish IDX trading closures. Each calendar retains its local timezone and source-specific limits.
