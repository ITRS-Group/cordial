# India (IN) holiday calendars

Snapshot gathered **2026-11-20**. See the [overall holiday calendar guide](README.md) for date formats, coverage limits, source-confidence grades and Geneos scheduling examples.

Gateway include: `holidays.layer0.in.xml`. The **Layer 0 - IN - India** group holds city-specific bank calendars and the NSE cash-market calendar, all using `Asia/Kolkata`.

The RBI and NSE lists describe different closures. These calendars are not a national public-holiday list or a substitute for state government-office schedules. All overrides are dated for 2026; no annually recurring dates or unverified future years are added.

## Source research and coverage

The [RBI holiday matrix](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/HolidayMatrixDisplay.aspx) provides regional-office bank lists for the three centres. Each **All Months / 2026** result was retrieved and its dated rows are encoded above. RBI sometimes uses composite holiday labels spanning several regional observances: the office filter establishes which dates apply, rather than implying that every holiday name in a label applies to that city.

| Centre | Jurisdiction | RBI regional-office selector | Verified dated rows | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chennai | Tamil Nadu | `16` | 23 | Medium; official HTML |
| Mumbai | Maharashtra | `28` | 22 | Medium; official HTML |
| New Delhi | National Capital Territory of Delhi | `30` | 16 | Medium; official HTML |

The RBI page uses an ASP.NET form. Select the regional office, **All Months**, and **2026**, then submit. Its fields are `drRegionalOffice`, `drMonth` (`0` for All Months), and `drYear`. Automated retrieval needs the current hidden form state; query-string-only filtering was not verified.

The RBI year selector offered **2026 and earlier**, not 2027. The captured NSE cash-market records also cover 2026 only. No 2027-2031 dates have been verified from these sources; missing future coverage is not evidence that there are no holidays.

The source scopes are distinct:

- **City bank holidays:** RBI's matrix distinguishes Negotiable Instruments Act holidays, combined NI Act/RTGS holidays and banks' closing-of-accounts days. Every dated row in these three captured city lists is labelled as a Negotiable Instruments Act holiday, including 1 April's yearly bank-account closing. Those exact row classifications are retained, rather than assigning dates a different classification from the matrix legend or assuming RTGS is closed.

- **Regional public holidays:** Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu and Delhi public/government-office schedules need their own notifications. No complete official state public-holiday notification was verified here; RBI bank dates do not replace those schedules.

- **Exchange closures:** the NSE JSON retry succeeded and the `CM` cash-market calendar is now encoded. Its structured dates have High source-format confidence; interpreting the HTML Muhurat-session note has Medium confidence. The 8 November special session is not a full-day closure.

The New Delhi RBI page also contains office-access and working-hour notes; these are not full-day holidays. RBI's second/fourth-Saturday rule and ordinary Sunday closures belong in separate recurring scheduling rules, not inferred holiday-date entries.

The [BSE holiday page](https://www.bseindia.com/static/markets/marketinfo/listholi.aspx) returned a JavaScript application shell without a dated schedule in the fetched response. No BSE calendar is encoded. Neither the NSE cash-market calendar nor Mumbai's bank holidays establish BSE or other trading-segment closures.
